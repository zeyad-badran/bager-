# Future plan

Things we decided to do **later**, with the reason and the trigger that moves each one into a task spec.
Nothing here is built or approved for building yet. The product owner decides when a trigger has happened.

## 1. Stronger order claim (hardening of ADR-0006)
**Why:** v1 uses branch QR + order number alone (owner-accepted risk). The order number can be read, guessed or sequential.
**Plan, in the order we would add it** (all designed and tested in `docs/spikes/claim-token.md`):
1. **Pickup code:** an 8-digit one-time code generated by our app, shown on the cashier screen or a slip, typed with the number.
2. **Per-order QR:** the staff screen shows a QR that opens the order directly, nothing typed (needs a "show QR" feature on the staff board).
3. **Device invitations:** first phone invites others (short TTL, bounded use, revocable).
4. **Staff code:** one-time code for customers who lost their phone or receipt.
5. **Per-restaurant setting:** let a restaurant use its POS's long random order number as the code, or our own code.
6. **Minimum ticket rules:** warn or refuse ticket numbers shorter than a restaurant-chosen length.
**Trigger:** a client or their security reviewer asks for it, the Phase 8 security review requires it, any abuse is seen
(guessing alerts), or the restaurant uses short or sequential numbers. Do not launch with a restaurant using 1-3 digit numbers
without at least step 1.
